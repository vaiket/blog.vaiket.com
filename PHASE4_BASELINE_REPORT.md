## Words per Markdown article

The long-form corpus is uneven by design: `ai-telecalling-in-india` is 26,200 words, the Ranchi guide is 32,083 words and the national ecosystem guide is 6,382 words. After Phase 5 enrichment and three actual batches, the original 25 priority drafts range from 523 to 1,363 words; new batch drafts are intentionally compact research pieces. All require claim-by-claim editorial approval based on usefulness, not padding for a word-count target.

## Baseline conclusion

The site has a working research corpus and a 300-topic plan, but it does not have 300 completed articles or 119 fully verified provider profiles. Phase 4 keeps these distinctions explicit.
