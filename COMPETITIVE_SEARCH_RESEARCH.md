# Competitive search research — Phase 3

Research date: 25 November 2026. Searches were run for India-focused WhatsApp API, RCS, AI calling, CPaaS, automation and comparison terms. Search snippets and third-party roundups were treated as discovery only; they are not proof of ranking, market share, price or product quality. No search volume, keyword difficulty or traffic number is claimed because no validated analytics export was available.

## Repeated search patterns

| Cluster | Results repeatedly surfaced | Opportunity for Vaiket |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp API providers India | AiSensy, WATI, Gupshup, Interakt, Gallabox, BIK, Twilio, 360dialog, Infobip and Route Mobile | Publish evidence-led buyer criteria, current source dates and a transparent unranked directory. |
| WhatsApp pricing India | Provider landing pages, Meta pricing explanations and agency roundups | Separate Meta conversation charges, BSP/platform fees, tax, support and implementation. |
| RCS providers India | Google RCS partner directory, CPaaS providers and aggregator pages | Build a partner-verification workflow; do not infer Indian availability from a global page. |
| AI calling cost India | Vendor blogs, cloud telephony pages and community comparisons | Use an editable model with connected minutes, transfer, telephony and human-review assumptions. |
| Migration and alternatives | AiSensy/WATI comparison and migration content | Create checklists around number ownership, templates, exports, consent and rollback. |
| AI business OS / MSME automation | Broad software-category pages with weak India-specific operational detail | Own practical workflow, compliance and integration guides rather than unsupported category leadership. |

## Editorial gap list

1. Most pages blend provider fees with Meta/carrier charges; Vaiket should show the cost layers separately.
2. “Best” pages rarely explain evidence dates, testing scope, ownership of the WhatsApp number or exit/migration risk.
3. India-specific compliance, language coverage, support escalation and data handling are under-explained.
4. Directory pages often imply ranking. Vaiket’s directory remains public, unranked and evidence-led.
5. Comparison pages need a visible corrections process and a “not independently tested” label where applicable.
