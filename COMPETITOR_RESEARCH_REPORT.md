# Competitor research report

The provider directory at `/research/providers/india` contains 25 selected provider or brand records from the supplied research file. It is unranked, incomplete and noindex. It does not imply certification, endorsement, partnership, pricing accuracy or independent performance testing.

The directory uses structured data from `content/provider-directory-india.md`, supports search and segment filtering, and displays only source links supplied in the research file. Individual provider pages have not been generated, preventing thin or duplicate indexable pages.

Future comparisons require current official product documentation, pricing/rate-card dates, legal-entity and parent-group checks, India availability and comparable testing. Vaiket is not automatically declared the winner.
