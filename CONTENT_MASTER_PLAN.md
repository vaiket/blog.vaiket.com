# Vaiket content master plan

Status: Phase 3 programme established; national 300-topic planning inventory is generated, while article bodies remain editorial work items.

The maintainable source of truth is `content/content-inventory.json` for article records, supplemented by `content/national-topic-programme.ts` for the 300-topic planning universe. The inventory records all 10 existing article records and 25 priority topics; all 25 priority topics have source-linked draft bodies. Planned topics are not fake placeholder pages and do not enter the sitemap.

The long-term target is 300 individually researched articles across WhatsApp Business API, AI Telecalling/Voice AI, RCS, Agentic AI/Automation, comparisons, industry solutions, Generative Commerce, Cloud/Developer Technologies and MSME growth. Targets are editorial planning targets, not completed metrics.

## Publication rules

- Use official documentation, regulators, standards, research papers and provider sources where possible.
- Record research cutoff and verification date.
- Separate provider-reported descriptions, editorial analysis, illustrative examples and verified Vaiket capabilities.
- Keep drafts out of the public sitemap and search index.
- Do not create thin competitor doorway pages or unverified rankings.
