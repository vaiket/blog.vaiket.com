# RCS Business Messaging Agent Verification in India: What to Check

*Research snapshot: 26 November 2026.*

**Editorial disclosure:** RCS reach, approval, billing and carrier behaviour are deployment-specific. This article explains an acceptance process; it does not claim that a provider or agent is approved in India.

## Verification is more than a logo

An RCS agent represents a business identity in a supported messaging client. Verify the brand name, logo, description, domains, phone or action destinations, owner, operator route and escalation contact. A screenshot of a rich card is not evidence of live reach or carrier coverage.

Google's [RCS Business Messaging overview](https://developers.google.com/business-communications/rcs-business-messaging/) describes agents, messages, events and API concepts. The [how-it-works guide](https://developers.google.com/business-communications/rcs-business-messaging/guides/get-started/how-it-works) explains the message and webhook flow and notes that SMS fallback may be used when a device does not support RCS. Confirm the current regional process with the selected provider.

## Agent verification checklist

| Area | Acceptance question |
|---|---|
| Identity | Does the visible identity match the legal business and approved brand? |
| Access | Are roles, keys and environment separation documented? |
| Content | Are rich cards, media, suggestions and URLs validated? |
| Events | Are delivery, read, reply, failure and opt-out events received? |
| Reach | Which devices, carriers and regions were tested? |
| Fallback | Is the SMS path approved, measurable and policy-compliant? |

## Launch a controlled pilot

Use a small consented cohort. Test new conversation, reply, media, carousel, action button, expired link, unsupported device, handset change, duplicate event and fallback. Keep a human support route. Record the agent version, provider, carrier, device, timestamp and event ID so a failure can be reproduced.
