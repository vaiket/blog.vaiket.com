# Free WhatsApp Business API: What Is Actually Free?

*Research snapshot: 25 November 2026.*

**Editorial disclosure:** Pricing changes frequently and depends on destination, message type, provider contract and tax. This guide does not claim a free tier for Vaiket or any competitor. Use Meta's [current pricing documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing) and the provider's written quote before budgeting.

## The word “free” hides several different things

| Claim | What it may mean | What still needs checking |
|---|---|---|
| Free API | No separate API license | Meta message charges, hosting and engineering remain |
| Free onboarding | No setup fee | Verification, number, template and support conditions |
| Free trial | Limited time or volume | Seats, integrations, overages and data export |
| Free conversation | A specific policy or service window | Category, country, date and policy eligibility |
| Free inbox | No dashboard fee at one tier | Message charges, limits and support |

The [Meta Business Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/overview) distinguishes the platform's API capabilities from provider software. A provider can add a subscription even when Meta does not charge an API licence fee.

## Build a real monthly model

Count customer-initiated sessions, business-initiated marketing or utility messages, active agents, templates, integrations, media storage, support and implementation. Record the country of the recipient and the purpose of every message. Then separate:

1. Meta charges.
2. Provider subscription and seats.
3. Optional automation or AI add-ons.
4. Hosting, integration and monitoring.
5. Tax and internal operating time.

The [WhatsApp pricing guide](/posts/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india) contains a workload model. A free dashboard is not free if a team spends hours repairing failed webhooks or cannot export its customer evidence.

## Common red flags

- “Unlimited” messages without a category, route or policy definition.
- No written answer about who owns the WABA and phone number.
- A single bundled price with no Meta/provider split.
- A free trial that requires a provider-controlled number.
- No opt-out, webhook, audit or export documentation.
- Claims that bypass template approval or customer consent.

## What you can reduce safely

Use Meta's Cloud API or a provider that gives clear account control. Start with one workflow. Keep message templates narrow and useful. Cache business data rather than asking the model or team to repeat work. Measure resolution, opt-out and cost per outcome. Do not reduce the budget for consent, security, monitoring or recovery.

## A safe answer for a small business

“Free” can mean a free software plan, a trial, a limited support tier or no platform fee for a narrow use case. It does not automatically mean free Meta conversations, free telecom delivery, free templates, free onboarding or free support. Put every zero-cost claim beside its boundary: number of users, messages, templates, automation steps, retention, export and duration. A transparent low-cost plan is more useful than a headline free label that changes after the first campaign.
