# Best WhatsApp Marketing Software in India: Consent, Campaigns and Measurement

*Research snapshot: 25 November 2026.*

**Editorial disclosure:** This guide is not a ranking. Vaiket publishes it and operates in business communication. Provider features and prices are time-sensitive; confirm the current Meta and provider terms for the campaign, recipient country, category and account.

## The best campaign is the one you can govern

Marketing software should make it easier to send relevant messages to people who have opted in—not merely make bulk sending faster. Meta's [WhatsApp Business Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/overview) covers templates, APIs, webhooks and pricing concepts. Meta's [business policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy) should be treated as a launch dependency, not a footer link.

## A buying matrix

| Capability | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| Consent | Source, timestamp, purpose, scope and suppression state |
| Segmentation | Audiences based on durable business fields, not guesswork |
| Templates | Approval, language, variable validation and version history |
| Frequency | Cooldowns, quiet hours, caps and campaign exclusions |
| Operations | Shared inbox, roles, assignments and human escalation |
| Measurement | Delivery, read, response, opt-out, complaint and outcome events |
| Portability | WABA, number, contacts, logs and template export path |

## India-specific questions

Ask how the vendor handles Indian phone formats, consent language, Hindi and code-mixed copy, regional festivals, quiet hours, opt-outs and support escalation. For SMS and voice campaigns, TRAI's [TCCCPR resources](https://www.trai.gov.in/tcccpr) explain the customer-preference framework; channel-specific rules and provider policies still need review.

## Campaign design that survives contact with customers

Start with one clear outcome: an opted-in customer receives a useful offer, asks a question, or completes a purchase. Exclude recent purchasers, support escalations, opt-outs and people outside the purpose of consent. Use a template that states the offer, expiry and next action. Provide a human support path and stop a campaign when complaints or delivery quality deteriorate.

Track incremental outcome against a control or historical baseline where possible. Do not call delivered messages revenue. The minimum dashboard should show delivered, read, reply, opt-out, complaint, conversion event, cost and time-to-resolution.

## Campaign QA before send

Use a holdout list and a small consented test group. Check template language, variables, links, opt-out behavior, quiet hours, frequency caps, delivery reporting and the human support path. A campaign platform should make it possible to explain why a person was contacted and to suppress future promotional messages across the relevant workflow. Measure complaints and support load, not only clicks.
