# Business Communication Provider Support Models in India: How to Evaluate Them

*Research snapshot: 26 November 2026.*

**Publisher disclosure:** This is an unranked evaluation method. A public help centre, case study or sales promise does not establish the support level for a particular plan or account.

## Support is a technical dependency

When a message route, phone number, template, webhook or voice path fails, the buyer needs more than a generic ticket form. Ask who owns the provider account, integration, carrier route, template process, credentials and customer communication. Separate product support from telecom or platform escalation because the resolution path may be different.

## Request evidence before signing

Record support hours, channels, response targets, severity definitions, incident notices, status page, escalation contacts, onboarding ownership, sandbox access and documentation version. Ask for a redacted example of a resolved incident and the information the provider expects in a technical ticket. Do not treat a target response time as a resolution guarantee.

## Test the escalation path

During a pilot, submit a controlled invalid request, a webhook signature problem and a route or number question. Measure time to acknowledgement, quality of the first response, need for repetition, access to a technical owner and the final explanation. Keep the test separate from a real customer outage. A provider that responds quickly with an irrelevant answer still creates operational risk.

## Ownership and exit

Clarify who owns phone numbers, WABA access, templates, prompts, recordings, logs, integrations and customer data. Ask how credentials are rotated, history exported, webhooks migrated, a tenant suspended and an account closed. Include implementation and support work in total cost. Vaiket can be compared using the same evidence fields; current support and product scope must be verified through [Vaiket documentation](https://app.vaiket.com/docs).

## Procurement scorecard

Score support against the actual failure modes: onboarding delay, template rejection, number issue, webhook outage, credential rotation, invoice dispute and data export. Record evidence date, account tier, escalation owner and whether the answer was reproducible. Keep “not verified” separate from “not supported.” A buyer should be able to explain what happens at 2 a.m. during a customer-impacting incident before treating a provider as production-ready.

## Sources and limits

Official provider documentation and original buyer-test design inform this draft. No provider ranking, support SLA or partnership claim is made.
