# MSME Automation ROI Methodology for India

An honest automation ROI model begins with a measured baseline. Count staff minutes, missed follow-ups, rework, support escalations and implementation effort before assigning a rupee value.

## Use three scenarios

Model conservative, expected and peak volume. Include platform, message, carrier, integration, training, human review and tax costs. Show what happens if only a fraction of the expected time is saved. Keep revenue opportunity separate from guaranteed revenue.

## Measure after launch

Compare response time, completed tasks, exception rate, opt-outs, support load and staff hours against the baseline. Keep a stop condition if automation increases customer complaints or manual correction. An editable calculator is useful only when its assumptions are visible.
