# AiSensy vs Wati in India: A Fair WhatsApp Platform Comparison Method

*Research snapshot: 26 November 2026.*

**Publisher disclosure:** Vaiket publishes this comparison and operates in the same category. It is not a ranking, live benchmark or endorsement. Prices and features must be confirmed with both providers.

## Compare operating models

AiSensy and Wati should be compared on the exact workflow, not the number of dashboard buttons. Ask who controls the WhatsApp Business Account and number, how templates and webhooks are handled, which seats and automation limits apply, how support escalates, what data exports exist and how a business leaves.

Use AiSensy's [official pricing page](https://aisensy.com/pricing) and Wati's [pricing-structure help article](https://support.wati.io/en/articles/11462993-understanding-wati-s-pricing-structure) as dated provider sources. They are not neutral market indexes and may change.

## Buyer test pack

Run the same onboarding, template, shared-inbox, webhook, export, opt-out and invoice-reconciliation tests. Record the date, plan, tax treatment, message charges, limits and support response. If a capability is not documented, mark it “verify,” not “no.”

## Test the operating model

Use the same script in both trials: connect a test number, create a template, send a utility message, receive a reply, trigger a webhook, export an interaction, add an operator, remove consent and reconcile an invoice. Record the date, plan or quote, region, included seats, message limits, onboarding work and support response. Public pricing pages are starting evidence, not a guarantee for a specific account.

Separate platform subscription, provider or conversation charges, implementation and human operations. Compare API surface, template workflow, role permissions, webhook retries, export capability, retention and migration path alongside the user interface. Ask whether numbers, templates, opt-out history, conversation context, webhooks and analytics can be moved without a dead period.

Vaiket should be evaluated with the same test pack, including documented APIs and actual support response. This keeps the comparison evidence-led rather than turning a brand mention into an unsupported recommendation.

## Where Vaiket fits

Vaiket can be included as a third option when a connected communication and automation workflow is relevant. Current Vaiket capabilities and pricing must be checked in [API docs](https://app.vaiket.com/docs) and a product review. No superiority claim is made.

## Sources and limits

Official provider pages and original comparison methodology support this guide. No customer survey or controlled product test was performed.
