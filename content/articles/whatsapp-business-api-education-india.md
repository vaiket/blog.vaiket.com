# WhatsApp Business API for Education in India

Education teams can use WhatsApp for enquiry routing, appointment reminders, document checklists and support, but student or parent communication needs clear ownership and access controls.

## Start with low-risk workflows

Route an enquiry to admissions, send a requested checklist, remind a confirmed appointment and provide a human support path. Keep sensitive student records out of broad campaign exports. Use role-based access and a retention policy suitable for the institution's context.

## Measure service quality

Track response time, unresolved conversations, handoffs and opt-outs. Do not treat message volume as engagement. Test a parent using a shared family number, a language switch and a request outside the published support scope.

Vaiket's [API docs](https://app.vaiket.com/docs) can support an integration review; confirm current roles, retention, number ownership and pricing before adoption.
