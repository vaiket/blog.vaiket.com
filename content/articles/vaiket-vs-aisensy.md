# Vaiket vs AiSensy: A Fair Evaluation Framework for WhatsApp Automation

*Research snapshot: 25 November 2026.*

**Publisher disclosure:** Vaiket publishes this comparison and is the subject of one side of it. It is not an independent ranking, paid review or controlled product benchmark. AiSensy features are described from public material; Vaiket claims are limited to verified positioning and must be confirmed in current product documentation.

## Start with the real decision

The question is not “which dashboard has more buttons?” It is “which operating model can this team run safely?” A small business may need fast WhatsApp onboarding and campaigns. A growing team may need assignment, approvals, templates, CRM events and analytics. A developer may care about webhooks, API credentials, data export and account portability.

Meta remains the platform authority. Its [WhatsApp Business Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/overview) covers Cloud API, templates, webhooks, authentication and pricing concepts. Both vendors sit around that platform; the commercial and operational layer is what should be compared.

## Side-by-side evaluation

| Criterion | AiSensy: verify from public/current terms | Vaiket: verify before relying on it |
|---|---|---|
| Core job | WhatsApp engagement, campaigns and automation are publicly described | WhatsApp communication, workflows and AI-enabled business operations are the stated direction |
| Onboarding | Confirm WABA/number ownership and Embedded Signup path | Confirm WABA/number ownership, onboarding responsibility and support path |
| Team operations | Check seats, roles, inbox and assignments | Check current collaboration, roles, approvals and audit history |
| Developer layer | Check webhooks, API, integrations and event limits | Check API documentation, webhooks, integrations and export path |
| Commercial model | Review [AiSensy pricing](https://www.aisensy.com/pricing) and Meta charges separately | Ask for a current written quote; this article does not invent Vaiket pricing |
| Exit plan | Confirm templates, contacts, logs and number migration | Confirm the same artifacts and recovery process |

The table deliberately uses “verify.” A feature page can change, a plan can limit it, and Meta approval or policy can be a separate dependency.

## What to test in a two-week pilot

### Onboarding and ownership

Use an authorised test account. Record who controls the Meta Business Account, WhatsApp Business Account, phone number, templates, tokens and recovery contacts. Do not let a convenience setup become an undocumented lock-in.

### Workflow depth

Test an inbound lead, a template-initiated follow-up, a human handoff, an opt-out and a failed webhook. Measure the number of manual steps and whether the team can explain the final state of every conversation.

### Cost clarity

Separate provider subscription, seats, add-ons, Meta message charges, implementation and tax. A monthly plan comparison without message volume and support assumptions is incomplete. Use the [WhatsApp pricing guide](/posts/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india) for a workload model.

### Reliability and portability

Look for retries, idempotency, event logs, export, rate limits and incident communication. Ask how a business can migrate without losing its number, approved templates, consent evidence or customer context.

## Where Vaiket may fit

Vaiket's verified positioning is a broader business communication and automation direction: WhatsApp workflows, AI-powered communication, team collaboration and developer integrations, with AI telecalling, RCS, Agentic AI and generative commerce as areas that require capability-by-capability confirmation. This is not a claim that every listed capability is live in every account.

Choose Vaiket when the required workflow, account model, support path and current integrations are documented for the business. Choose AiSensy when its current onboarding, campaign and operational fit is the better evidence-backed match. The fair result can differ by team.

## Questions neither demo should skip

- Who owns and can recover the WABA and phone number?
- Which message events are available through webhooks?
- What are the export and offboarding steps?
- How are opt-outs, roles and audit logs handled?
- Which costs are provider fees versus Meta charges?
- What happens when a policy, template or quality status changes?

## Keep the comparison fair

Use a shared test tenant or a documented sandbox and record the test date. Compare onboarding effort, number ownership, template workflow, operator roles, API behavior, export, support response and the total invoice—not only a feature list. Mark a capability as “not verified” when the public page does not show the exact plan, country or limit. Vaiket is the publisher of this comparison, so the article should invite corrections and keep a visible distinction between public documentation and editorial judgment.

The current Vaiket evidence set is [API documentation](https://app.vaiket.com/docs), [India pricing](https://www.vaiket.com/in/pricing) and the [WhatsApp product page](https://www.vaiket.com/product/whatsapp). These are useful starting points, not independent performance tests.
