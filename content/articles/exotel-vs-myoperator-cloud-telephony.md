# Exotel vs MyOperator in India: Cloud Telephony Evaluation Framework

*Research snapshot: 26 November 2026.*

**Publisher disclosure:** This is an unranked evaluation guide. Public feature pages do not prove route authorisation, quality, support or price for a specific Indian deployment.

## Map the layers

Compare number provisioning, KYC, inbound IVR, outbound calling, queues, recordings, APIs, webhooks, SIP, CRM integration, analytics, AI handoff and support separately. A business may need one provider for telecom connectivity and another for workflow or AI logic.

Exotel's [vSIP documentation](https://support.exotel.com/support/solutions/articles/3000133452-flow-and-api-configuration-guide-for-voice-ai-contact-centre-platforms-via-exotel-virtual-sip-trunk) gives provider-specific technical evidence. MyOperator's [cloud telephony page](https://myoperator.com/cloud-telephony) describes its product area. Neither page is an independent comparison.

## Pilot checklist

Test number activation, caller ID, business-hours routing, queue overflow, callback, webhook retries, recording access, CRM updates, human transfer and invoice reconciliation. Ask for written limits and data-retention terms.

## Pilot and decision matrix

Run the pilot with a fixed script and known numbers. Test provisioning, caller ID, business-hours routing, queue overflow, callback, agent login, transfer, recording access, webhook retries, CRM write-back, SIP or API integration and invoice reconciliation. Capture configuration and test time because telecom behavior can vary by route and account.

Score only requirements that matter to the deployment: KYC process, number coverage, inbound and outbound use case, API documentation, event delivery, recording controls, support escalation, analytics export, disaster recovery and contract exit. Mark “not tested” separately from “does not support.” Do not infer nationwide coverage from marketing language.

Voice connectivity, call control, speech recognition, business logic and human support are separate layers. Ask where recordings and transcripts are processed, how transfer context is passed, and who owns a failure when a call connects but the workflow does not.

## Vaiket boundary

Vaiket may fit when communication and automation need to connect, but live voice routes and integrations require product confirmation. Do not infer a Vaiket telecom capability from this comparison.

## Sources and limits

Official Exotel and MyOperator material plus original test design inform this guide. No vendor ranking or benchmark is claimed.
