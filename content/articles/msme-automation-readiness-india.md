# MSME Automation Readiness Checklist for India

Automation works best when the business can name the work, the owner, the input, the expected outcome and the exception. A small Indian team does not need a giant transformation programme to begin; it needs one workflow that can be measured and stopped safely.

## Readiness questions

Is the customer or lead data consistent? Does every team member know who owns a follow-up? Is consent recorded? Can a human correct a wrong classification? Can the business export its records? Can it explain what happens when an API, payment or carrier is unavailable? If the answer is no, improve the process before adding autonomy.

## A first workflow

Start with an inbound enquiry. Capture the source, match or create a lead, assign an owner, send one relevant response and create a task. Keep price changes, refunds, financial commitments and sensitive data behind approval. Measure response time, missed follow-ups, human corrections and customer opt-outs.
