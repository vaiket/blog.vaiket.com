# AI Calling CRM Webhooks for Indian Sales Teams

The useful output of an AI call is not only a transcript. It is a disposition, a next action, an owner, a confidence or transfer reason and an audit trail that a human can correct.

## Keep writes idempotent

Use a call ID and event type as the primary key. A retry should update the existing disposition, not create a second lead or task. Separate “call completed” from “qualified lead” and never infer the latter from duration alone.

## Make the human path explicit

Show the operator the transcript excerpt, extracted fields and reason for transfer. Require approval before discounts or commitments. Test duplicate callbacks, invalid CRM IDs, a customer who opts out and a CRM outage during call completion.
