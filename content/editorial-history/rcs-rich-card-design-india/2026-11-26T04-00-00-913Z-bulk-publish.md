# RCS Rich Card Design for Indian Customer Journeys

Rich cards should reduce a decision to a clear next action. They should not be a miniature website that fails when images, handset support or network conditions change.

## Design the action hierarchy

Use one primary action, concise copy, a meaningful image and a fallback label. Confirm whether the action needs authentication or a human. Test long Indian names, rupee amounts, Hindi-English copy and a missing image. Keep the same campaign and customer event ID across RCS and fallback channels.

## Measure customer outcomes

Record delivery, read, action tap, fallback, reply, opt-out and support escalation separately. Do not infer purchase intent from a tap. Verify RCS capability and partner/carrier reach for the exact audience before committing campaign spend.
