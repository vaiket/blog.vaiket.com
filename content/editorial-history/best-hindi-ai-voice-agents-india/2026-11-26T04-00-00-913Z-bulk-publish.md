# Best Hindi AI Voice Agents in India: How to Test Language Quality

*Research snapshot: 25 November 2026.*

**Editorial disclosure:** The “best” agent depends on accent, task, route, vocabulary, latency and human fallback. This guide is not a language benchmark or ranking. Use consented or synthetic test calls and verify provider terms.

## Hindi is not one test case

Test native Devanagari speech, Hinglish, regional accents, fast speech, background noise, names, addresses, amounts, dates, vehicle numbers and code-switched product terms. A system can sound fluent and still fail the business task by changing a digit or a proper noun.

Sarvam's [building-for-India documentation](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/building-for-india) distinguishes transcription, translation, transliteration, code-mix and telephony audio. Its [voice-agent documentation](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/build/models) describes Indian-language coverage, Bulbul voices and code-mixed input. These sources establish product scope, not comparative accuracy for every route.

## A language-quality test pack

1. Read a consent statement and capture opt-out.
2. Ask a customer to spell a name and repeat it back.
3. Speak a phone number, amount and date in Hindi and Hinglish.
4. Interrupt the agent mid-sentence and correct one detail.
5. Ask an out-of-scope question and require a safe handoff.
6. Compare transcript, spoken response and CRM field values.

Score entity accuracy and task completion separately from word error rate. Record the model, prompt, language mode, audio codec, provider route and date.

## Voice safety

The agent should identify itself appropriately, provide a human option, respect opt-outs and avoid sensitive disclosure. Review TRAI's [TCCCPR resources](https://www.trai.gov.in/tcccpr) and the [DoT telecom cyber-security portal](https://www.eservices.dot.gov.in/telecom-cyber-security) for current frameworks; seek legal advice for the campaign.

## Hindi voice test pack

Include formal Hindi, colloquial Hindi, Hinglish, regional pronunciation, names, dates, currency, addresses, phone digits, interruptions and background television. Ask the same caller to switch language mid-conversation and to correct the agent. Score transcription, intent, pronunciation, latency and safe escalation separately. Do not report one accuracy percentage unless the dataset, audio conditions, language variety and scoring method are published.

Vaiket's current public evidence does not establish a Hindi voice benchmark. The [AI telecalling research](/posts/ai-telecalling-in-india) provides a testing framework; any live Vaiket language availability must be confirmed for the intended route.
