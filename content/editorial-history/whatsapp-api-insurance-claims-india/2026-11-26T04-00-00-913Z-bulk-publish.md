# WhatsApp Business API for Insurance Claims in India: Status, Documents and Human Support

*Research snapshot: 26 November 2026.*

**Editorial disclosure:** This is a technical workflow guide, not insurance, legal or claims advice. Insurers and intermediaries must apply their own sector rules, policy terms and privacy controls.

## Keep the claim system authoritative

WhatsApp should communicate a claim event; it should not become the claim ledger. Use a stable claim reference, policy-safe display name, event version, assigned team, preferred language and next action. The claim system remains authoritative for status, admissibility, surveyor assignment, document requirements, settlement and closure.

Meta's [WhatsApp Business Platform documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/overview) separates messages, templates and webhooks. An implementation should persist the business event before sending, then reconcile the provider response and status webhook without creating duplicate notices.

## Minimal disclosure and document handling

A notification can say that a claim status changed and provide a secure link to the authorised portal. Avoid exposing policy numbers, medical details, vehicle documents or settlement amounts in a shared-device notification. If a customer uploads a document, record the claim reference, access role, retention rule and malware or format check. Do not store sensitive files in a public media bucket or a message log by default.

## Exceptions to test

Test an incorrect phone number, family member answering, duplicate claim event, stale status, missing document, link expiry, opt-out, language change and a customer asking for a settlement decision. Route disputes, suspected fraud, vulnerable-customer needs and complaints to an authorised human queue. If a webhook is delayed, show a safe “being verified” state rather than a guessed status.

## Buyer questions

Ask providers about role-based access, audit export, document retention, webhook signatures, retry behavior, human handoff, tenant isolation and number migration. Measure duplicate-message rate, status freshness, document-request completion, unresolved queue age and escalation success. Do not infer claim settlement improvement from message delivery alone.

## Pilot evidence

Review one successful, one delayed, one duplicate and one escalated claim. Check that the operator can see the claim event, message version, document status, customer preference and next action without opening unrestricted clinical or financial data. Test a policy update and a number change before allowing automated reminders to run at scale.
