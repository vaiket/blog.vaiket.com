# WhatsApp Business API for Indian Local Services: Lead Capture and Booking Workflow

*Research snapshot: 26 November 2026.*

**Editorial disclosure:** This guide describes an operational pattern for local services; it does not claim lead volume, conversion or business growth for any vendor.

## Start with serviceability

The first automated question should change the next action: service type, location or pincode, preferred time, urgency and a callback preference. Keep the answer separate from the customer's free-form description. A business should be able to see why a lead was accepted, rejected or sent to a human.

Use Meta's [WhatsApp Business Platform documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/overview) for platform concepts, then connect the conversation to the authoritative booking or CRM system. Do not rely on a spreadsheet export as the only source of truth.

## Consent and handoff

Record acquisition source, purpose, timestamp, language, opt-out state and owner. A customer who asks for a quote may not have consented to unlimited promotional follow-ups. Stop or reduce outreach after an opt-out and show the customer how to contact the business again.

Create a human handoff for unsafe work, price exceptions, complaints, unclear addresses, a service area outside policy or a customer requesting a specific technician. Pass lead ID, summary, preferred time and consent state—not an unsupported “hot lead” label presented as fact.

## Scheduling and recovery

Prevent duplicate bookings with an idempotency key based on lead and slot. Re-check slot availability immediately before confirmation. If payment or calendar integration fails, show a pending state and create an owner task instead of asking the customer to repeat the action. Test reschedule, cancellation, wrong number, shared phone and no-show flows.

Measure qualified-field completeness, time to first human response, duplicate booking rate, unresolved queue age, opt-out correctness and no-show recovery. Avoid using message count as a growth metric.

## A useful lead record

Store source, service type, serviceability result, preferred slot, language, consent state, owner, last customer message and next action. Keep free-form customer context separate from structured fields. If an address or slot is uncertain, create a human task rather than confirming a visit that the business cannot fulfil.
