# AI Lead Qualification for Indian Real Estate: WhatsApp and Voice Workflow

*Research snapshot: 26 November 2026.*

**Editorial disclosure:** Lead quality and conversion vary by market, inventory and sales process. This is a workflow design, not a conversion claim.

## Ask only what changes the next action

Capture project or service area, purpose, timeline, budget range, preferred language, contact permission and next step. Do not infer eligibility or promise availability. Read inventory, price and possession data from an authoritative system and show the last-updated time to the operator.

The [WhatsApp Business Platform overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/overview) covers templates and webhooks; voice routes require separate telecom and consent verification. Use one customer record for both channels and persist source, event ID and owner.

## Human handoff

Transfer when a prospect asks for a site visit, finance detail, legal document, complaint or negotiated quote. Give the salesperson a concise context packet and let them correct the qualification state. Suppress contacts who opt out and prevent duplicate follow-ups.

## Handoff and integration design

Create a handoff when the prospect asks for a site visit, financing or legal commitment, gives contradictory information, requests an unsupported language, or objects to continued outreach. Pass transcript summary, source campaign, requested property, volunteered budget range, location, next action and consent state. Never pass an invented high-intent label as a customer statement.

Deduplicate by a controlled identity key and keep the original source event. Store each answer with a timestamp; do not overwrite an earlier answer without recording the correction. Write to the CRM only after validating field formats and ownership. If the CRM is unavailable, queue the result rather than losing the lead or creating duplicates.

Property availability, price, possession date and financing information change frequently. Pull them from an authoritative source with a freshness timestamp and route stale records to human review. Measure field completeness, handoff success, stale-answer rate, duplicate rate and opt-out handling—not just lead volume.
