# Best SIP Trunk Providers in India: How to Compare Capacity, Security and Route

*Research snapshot: 25 November 2026.*

**Editorial disclosure:** “Best” depends on route, traffic, capacity, support and regulatory fit. This article does not grant telecom authorisation or rank vendors. Confirm current licensing, contract terms, number allocation and permitted traffic with the provider.

## SIP trunk in one paragraph

SIP is the signalling protocol used to establish and manage calls; RTP carries media. A SIP trunk connects a business PBX, contact centre or voice AI gateway to a telecom network. RFC 3261 is the primary [SIP specification](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3261), while RFC 3550 defines the [RTP framework](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3550). Production design also needs NAT, firewall, codec, failover, monitoring and lawful route decisions.

## Technical selection checklist

### Transport and media

Ask whether the provider supports SIP over TLS and SRTP, which certificates are used, which codecs are permitted, packet-loss handling, DTMF, early media and hold/transfer behaviour. For Indian PSTN audio, test PCMA/PCMU through the actual route.

### Capacity and burst

Define concurrent calls, calls per second, outbound attempts per minute, answer rate and peak duration. A provider's maximum is not the safe capacity for a campaign. Use a headroom policy and test queue overflow.

### Number and compliance

Ask how numbers are provisioned, what KYC is required, how caller identity is presented, how traffic is classified and what happens after a complaint. Review TRAI's [TCCCPR resources](https://www.trai.gov.in/tcccpr) and DoT's [telecom cyber-security portal](https://www.eservices.dot.gov.in/telecom-cyber-security).

## Capacity formula

For a rough steady-state estimate, `concurrent calls ≈ connected calls per minute × average duration in minutes`. Add burst headroom, failed attempts, transfers, callback traffic and provider limits. This is an engineering approximation, not a carrier quota.

## A minimal SIP readiness test

Validate registration/authentication, codec negotiation, DTMF, early media, transfer, hold, hang-up, RTP direction, TLS/SRTP where required and behavior under packet loss. Measure concurrent calls and post-dial delay separately. Keep SIP protocol correctness separate from the provider's India number, route and regulatory position; an RFC reference cannot prove commercial availability.

Use the calculator for a first capacity estimate, then confirm trunk limits and burst behavior with the provider. Vaiket's public [API documentation](https://app.vaiket.com/docs) is not a SIP-trunk specification, so any voice integration should remain a confirmation item.
