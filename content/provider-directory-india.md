## Research coverage and methodology

The 25 rows above are a starting set of provider brands with public source links. Eleven company LinkedIn pages are recorded; verify all links before publication. Google partner-directory entries are evidence of a public listing, not a substitute for checking a buyer's specific coverage and commercial agreement. No direct ranking is assigned. For detailed independent profiles, obtain primary product documentation, current rate cards, country coverage, contractual terms and comparable tests.

## Expanding toward 1,000 researched organizations

Define the universe before ranking: India-relevant providers of customer engagement, WhatsApp, voice AI, telecom connectivity, RCS, CRM, commerce and adjacent infrastructure. Do not mix these categories into a single numerical ranking without a meaningful common metric. Create separate segment views. For each candidate, establish an official website, legal name, brand name, parent entity, country, segment, evidence URL, LinkedIn URL if confirmed, date checked, India availability, and whether claims are vendor-reported or independently tested.

Discovery sources include Meta's official developer and partner resources, Google's RCS partner directory, company websites, public corporate filings, relevant stock-exchange disclosures, official telecom information, Startup India and industry associations. A listing on a startup database does not imply product certification. Do not scrape private personal information or invent entries to reach a quota. Deduplicate subsidiaries and product brands; retain parent relationships. Record failed verifications in a private research queue and exclude unverified records from the public directory.

For each public profile, describe the actual value proposition, ideal buyer to evaluate, known integration boundaries, primary source and last review date. Publish a corrections form. Offer providers the same evidence submission process whether or not they compete directly with Vaiket. Do not require a reciprocal link to be listed. A provider link is an outbound reference, not an inbound backlink for Vaiket.
